SOLARFREN X INTRO TEMPLATE
==========================

Replace the bracketed parts, then post this from your own X account:

I'm [your age].

[Your role] from [your location].

Looking to connect with more builders & indie hackers!

FLOW
----
1. Post your introduction.
2. Reply, like, and follow similar builder/founder posts.
3. Let X learn that this is the conversation you want; your feed should fill with more of it.
4. Run SolarFren on that feed. It scans normal posts, quoted posts, and Notifications.

SolarFren does not write posts or manufacture engagement. You create the signal;
the script helps you discover people in the feed you built.
